# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, constructive community for everyone who contributes or participates.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be considerate and direct.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and respect.
- Accept feedback and focus on improving the work.
- Protect private information and avoid harassment.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns through the repository's private contact or reporting options when available.
